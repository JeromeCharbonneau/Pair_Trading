KNOWN GAPS / CAVEATS
Identified on 2026-07-29 and unresolved at the time of the initial pull.

1. Point-in-time limitation
Yahoo Finance / yfinance returns the current continuous series as of the pull date.
Historical revisions, contract specifications, and back-adjustments are not locked
as of each historical date.

2. Survivorship and continuity limitation
The “=F” symbols are continuous front-month-style futures series spliced across
contract rolls. The roll method and adjustment conventions are vendor-defined and
not transparent in this dataset. Price levels and spreads may therefore be distorted
around roll dates.

3. Missing observations
There were 19 missing Close values across the downloaded series:

- Gold: 2
- Silver: 2
- Oats: 2
- Rice: 2
- Soybeans: 2
- Wheat: 2

These missing values were not investigated during the initial session. They may be
caused by holidays, trading halts, vendor gaps, or genuinely unavailable observations.

4. Log-price warning
At least one run of np.log produced an invalid-value warning. This suggests that
zero, negative, or otherwise problematic values may exist in the vendor data.
The issue was not fully audited during the initial session.

5. Universe-selection bias
The commodity universe was manually selected and focused on relatively liquid
contracts. There was no systematic inclusion rule. The pair-selection results are
therefore conditional on this convenience sample.

6. Execution-data limitation
The dataset does not include bid-ask spreads, volume, open interest, individual
contract-month identifiers, or explicit roll dates. It is therefore insufficient
for fully realistic transaction-cost and roll-aware execution analysis without a
later data upgrade.
